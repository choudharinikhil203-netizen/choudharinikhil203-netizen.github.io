GAME TOP – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GAME TOP, developed by Nikhil Devidas Choudhari, handles information when you use the GAME TOP Android application.

1. Information and Device Data

GAME TOP may access device information required to provide its features, including available RAM, battery status, storage information, display refresh-rate information, network status, CPU/device performance information, thermal information, and application/game information available through Android system APIs.

2. Game Library and Installed Applications

GAME TOP may inspect application information on your device to identify and display eligible games in the Game Library. GAME TOP does not need access to the private content, messages, photos, or files inside those applications.

3. Booster and Performance Features

When you use Boost or Quick Boost, GAME TOP may request Android system operations to clean up eligible background applications where Android permits it. GAME TOP reports measured information available through public Android APIs and does not guarantee a particular FPS, RAM improvement, or gaming performance result.

4. FPS Locker and Inspection

GAME TOP may inspect publicly available Android display and refresh-rate capabilities to help you evaluate FPS targets such as 30, 60, 90, and 120 FPS. GAME TOP does not claim to directly control another game's internal FPS when Android does not provide a public API for doing so.

5. Advertising

GAME TOP uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and ad interaction information according to Google's policies and the user's device and consent settings.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

GAME TOP may request Android permissions or special access only when required for a feature. Depending on the Android version and features used, these may include notification access, usage information, background-app management capabilities, network access, and foreground-service functionality. You can deny optional permissions, although related features may not work.

7. Data Storage and Sharing

GAME TOP does not operate a separate personal-data collection server for the core app features. Local settings and feature state may be stored on your device. Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to their applicable policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Uninstalling GAME TOP generally removes its local app data according to Android's standard app-data behavior. Advertising-related data may be retained by third-party providers according to their policies.

9. Children's Privacy

GAME TOP is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep the app and its local data handling appropriate for the intended features. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GAME TOP's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
